The system

10 steps from blank domain to booked meeting.

The same playbook runs behind every result above. More of it is automated in Claude Code each month, so hand-offs between steps don't depend on anyone doing them by hand.

Infrastructure setup

Domains, inboxes (typically 10 domains / 30 inboxes), warmup and platform config for deliverability.

Deep market research

Build the GTM context: market, buyers, competitors and triggers.

TAM mapping

Map industries and sub-industries by size, value and growth potential.

ICP validation

Prioritise segments, define personas and pin down core pains.

Account sourcing

Apollo, Clay, Ocean.io lookalikes and industry directories.

Targeting keywords

Precise keyword lists so database filters return exact-fit companies.

Signal-based lead gen

Job posts, LinkedIn activity and news triggers, then waterfall enrichment and verification.

Offers & messaging

Front-end offers and multiple spintax variants built on ICP pains and social proof.

Campaign execution

Launch within safe volume limits, then monitor replies and deliverability daily.

Booking & nurture

Handle positive replies, warm-call every meeting and nurture the rest.
